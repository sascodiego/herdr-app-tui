Tradeoff: the most native-mobile of the three — no overlay, no backdrop, the terminal never loses its top edge — and the only one where you can type into an agent while the list is open. Cost: expanding reflows and re-wraps the terminal, and the list is capped by the rows you are willing to take from the work area.

Fixes: P1 workspace once, in the header; P2 tabs carry their agent; P3 menu in one dim footer; P4 glyph plus word per agent; P5 nothing is covered, so there is no "back" to guess at.
