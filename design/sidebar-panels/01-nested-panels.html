01 — Nested panels

Two panels in the sidebar, not one tree: spaces with their tabs nested inside, and agents below. Every item owns two rows — name on the first, detail on the second — and the selection band covers both. This is the herdr sidebar cadence with terminal-native hierarchy: bold label, indented detail, one rule between groups, no boxes.

Tradeoff: the closest reading of the herdr sidebar and the two-row rule, but every space expanded means a long sheet when there are many tabs.

Fixes: P1 each space, tab and agent owns two full rows; P2 the selected band covers the pair, with a bold label and no marker glyph; P3 the space is a parent node and its tabs sit below it at a 4-cell indent; P4 the two panels are separated by one blank row, never by a nested box; P5 the active space shows only its branch because the band above already carries its path, while inactive spaces show `branch · path`; P6 state is always glyph plus word, never color alone; P7 keyboard focus has its own accent outline, distinct from selection.

Data source: branch comes from `folder_info`; `state · app` comes from the core's OSC 7501 status. No field is invented.

Palette: Gotham Default tokens from `design/system.md` only. No animation, so reduced-motion needs no exception.
