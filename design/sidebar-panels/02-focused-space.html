Tradeoff: the two-row rule stays, but a collapsed space hides its tabs until tapped. Fewer rows in the sheet; one extra tap to reach a tab in another space.

Fixes: P1 every item still owns exactly two rows; P2 the expanded space is the only parent that renders tab rows; P3 collapsed spaces keep their path and tab count on row two, so the space's identity never collapses to a single line; P4 agents gain an aligned time column on the same second row, without a third row; P5 the selection band still covers both rows and focus stays a separate outline.

Data source: time-in-state is derived from `Agent.since` in `src/model.rs:69-79`; it is not invented when the core reports `0`.

Palette: Gotham Default tokens from `design/system.md` only. No animation; reduced-motion needs no exception.
