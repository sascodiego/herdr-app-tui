03 — Rising peek

Opening the sheet no longer buries the terminal. Four rows of live output stay visible above the panel, so you can keep watching an agent while you pick the next one. The rising sheet carries its own one-column gutters and keeps the sidebar actions at its foot. Less room for the list; more context for the choice.
